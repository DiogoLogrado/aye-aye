# Changelog

Changes in each release, newest first. The installed app shows the entry for its new version after it updates, and each entry is the text of its GitHub release.

## 0.4.0

- After an update, the app shows what's new in that version.
- The "Update ready" message lists what's new, and waits until the app is on screen instead of appearing on another monitor while it's minimized.
- Update downloads show their progress on the taskbar icon.

## 0.3.0

- A loading screen with the Aye-aye logo shows while the app starts, and the window opens sooner.

## 0.2.0

- Automatic updates: new versions download in the background and install when you restart the app.
- **Help ▸ Check for updates…** checks right away.

## 0.1.0

- First release.
